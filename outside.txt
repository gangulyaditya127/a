5. MATURITY SCORING RULES
============================================================
 
Use the following maturity mapping:
 
Score 1:
- Label: Ad hoc
- Risk: High
- Interpretation: Capability is nominal, inconsistent, manually handled, or not outcome-linked.
 
Score 2:
- Label: Fragmented
- Risk: Medium-High
- Interpretation: Capability exists in pockets but is manual, inconsistent, or weakly governed.
 
Score 3:
- Label: Controlled with gaps
- Risk: Medium
- Interpretation: Capability is managed but has visible gaps in automation, quality, or scale.
 
Score 4:
- Label: Effective and improving
- Risk: Low
- Interpretation: Capability is operationally effective, improving, and linked to outcomes.
 
Decision Rules:
 
1. Treat "No" as a significant capability gap.
2. Treat "Partial" as an incomplete or inconsistent capability.
3. Treat "Manual" as a productivity and automation opportunity.
4. Treat maturity score 1 as Critical / Ad hoc.
5. Treat maturity score 2 as Needs Improvement / Fragmented.
6. Treat maturity score 3 as Moderate / Controlled with gaps.
7. Treat maturity score 4 as Good / Operationally effective.
8. If direct response and maturity rating conflict, flag as "Requires Validation".
9. If GenAI is marked false, do not claim AI is mature.
10. If ticket data quality is Poor, mark AI readiness as constrained.
11. If real-time SLA view is No, mark governance as reactive.
12. If tickets are not fully logged, mark KPI truthfulness as at risk.
13. If SRs are non-ticketed, mark demand visibility as distorted.
14. If ticket assignment is Manual, mark triage productivity as weak.
15. If build/testing is No, mark DevOps maturity as weak.
16. If deployment automation is Partial but maturity is low, mark deployment governance as fragmented.
 
Calculation Rules:
 
Calculate:
 
- Overall maturity average
- Domain-wise maturity average
- Service Management maturity
- Incident Management maturity
- Governance and Reporting maturity
- Service Request Fulfilment maturity
- Change Management maturity
- Automation maturity
- AI adoption maturity
- Modernization readiness
- Productivity risk
 
Use only numeric maturity ratings present in the JSON.
